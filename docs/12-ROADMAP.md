# Product Roadmap

## Phase 0
Repository, architecture, authentication, database, design system, testing foundation.

## Phase 1
Resume upload, parsing, profile review, screenshot upload, job extraction, and job review.

## Phase 2
Resume matching, email generation, validation, preview, Gmail OAuth, and send.

## Phase 3
Application dashboard, timeline, statuses, duplicate detection, analytics, polish.

## Phase 4
Multiple resume versions, browser extension, mobile share flow.
In-app follow-up reminders were brought forward from Phase 4 (change `personal-workflow-and-ui-polish`) because the Phase 2 send flow is in regular personal use. Follow-up email drafting/sending stays in Phase 4.

## Phase 5
Optional job discovery and career intelligence after validating repeated personal usage.

## Rule
Do not start Phase 4 or 5 until the Phase 2 vertical slice is stable and used regularly.
