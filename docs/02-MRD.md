# MRD — Market Requirements Document

## Problem signals
Target users discover jobs across LinkedIn, Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp groups, referrals, and company websites.
Existing job tools often optimize discovery, tracking, or mass application rather than screenshot-first application preparation.

## Personas
1. Software engineer actively searching for jobs.
2. Developer who saves job screenshots and forgets to apply.
3. Remote-job seeker who repeatedly writes similar application emails.

## Competitive categories
- Job platforms: discovery and native applications.
- Application trackers: organization and follow-up.
- Browser automation tools: application acceleration.
- Resume tools: optimization and keyword matching.

## Positioning
ApplyFlow AI is a screenshot-first application accelerator with human-controlled AI preparation and Gmail delivery.

## Differentiators
- Image-first input.
- Structured extraction.
- Resume-grounded personalization.
- Explicit human approval.
- Application lifecycle tracking.

## Market validation plan
Before monetization, measure repeated weekly usage, completed applications, time saved, email acceptance, interview outcomes, and willingness to pay.
