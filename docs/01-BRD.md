# BRD — Business Requirements Document

## Business problem
Job seekers frequently discover opportunities through social media and communities, save screenshots, and delay applications because the application workflow is repetitive.

## Business objective
Reduce the time and mental friction between discovering a relevant job and sending a high-quality application.

## Initial business model
The product is personal-first and portfolio-first. SaaS monetization is a later hypothesis, not an MVP requirement.

## Success metrics
- Time from screenshot upload to approved application under 3 minutes.
- At least 80% of valid uploaded jobs reach a user decision.
- Zero accidental email sends.
- Application history is complete enough to prevent repeated manual tracking.

## Future monetization
Potential plans may include free usage, paid AI application limits, resume intelligence, follow-up automation, and career analytics.
Pricing must be validated with real user behavior before implementation.

## Business risks
- Gmail permission friction.
- AI extraction errors.
- User trust around sending email.
- AI cost per application.
- Dependence on third-party providers.

## ROI hypothesis
The first ROI is recovered personal time and increased completed applications; monetary SaaS ROI is evaluated only after personal usage proves retention.
